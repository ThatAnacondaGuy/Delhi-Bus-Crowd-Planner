How to read this table
  • Crowded rider-minutes removed: minutes that riders spend in a crowded bus, which one extra bus on that departure would take away.
  • Riders moved down a level: riders who would go from crowded to standing, or from standing to seats.
  • The maximum load section is the stretch of the trip with the most people on board. The load factor is the people on board divided by what the bus is rated to carry (seats plus standing places), so 1.00 is a full bus and above 1.00 is crowded.
  • Evidence share: the share of the trip's stretches that rest on many app tickets rather than a rough guess.
  • Buses that ran come from GPS where coverage is 0.8 or more. Elsewhere they are estimates from the timetable. Pick a route to see the bus count behind every cell.
